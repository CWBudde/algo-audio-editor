verification, atomic commit and painted channel/overview waveforms. Extraction
includes binary transfer, destination import and painting. No Preview or
prepared candidate precedes the clock. The ten-minute stereo budget remains
**<1 s for every operation**. Host scheduling and load affect results; isolated
passes do not supersede the complete sweep's failures. Native/WASM numerical,
geometry, history and cancellation assertions remain mandatory.

## Reproduction and remaining acceptance

Run the production worker scenarios serially, on the target laptop with other
CPU-heavy work idle:

```sh
just bench-process-browser
```

This intentionally measures the unchanged gate, so it can fail. The scenarios
live in `apps/editor-web/e2e/process-benchmark.spec.ts`; native goldens live in
`packages/kernel/internal/process`. The latest complete historical sweep has
**17 passes / 15 timing failures**, despite all correctness assertions passing.
Nonlinear envelopes, generators, DC removal and quality-preserving FIR
resampling need further optimization. Do not reduce quality/taps, widen the
budget, prepare a candidate, or skip actual output verification. Phase 15
remains open; any new result should identify its commit, hardware and load.
